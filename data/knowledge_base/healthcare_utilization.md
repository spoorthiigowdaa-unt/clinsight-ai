# Healthcare Utilization Risk

Healthcare utilization refers to the use of healthcare services such as outpatient visits, emergency department visits, inpatient admissions, procedures, and medication-related services.

## Factors Associated with High Healthcare Utilization

High healthcare utilization may be associated with several factors, including:

- Multiple chronic conditions
- Frequent emergency department visits
- Previous inpatient admissions
- High procedure utilization
- Polypharmacy or high medication burden
- Complex care needs
- Poor continuity of care

## Risk Prediction

Risk prediction models can use historical healthcare utilization patterns and patient characteristics to identify patients who may require additional care management.

Historical encounters, conditions, procedures, and medication utilization can provide information about previous healthcare needs and utilization patterns.

## Responsible Use

Predictive models should not be treated as autonomous clinical decision systems.

Model predictions should support human review and should be interpreted together with relevant clinical context.

CareGuard AI is designed as a demonstration system using synthetic healthcare data and is not intended to provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.
